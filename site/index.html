// STAR CITIZEN CONTROL DECK

Your tablet becomes
a flight deck.

A HUD-style button deck in your tablet's or phone's browser. Every button presses real keys on your gaming PC: landing gear, quantum, shields, power, mining and more. New in 2.2: battle alerts from the game's log, 25 more buttons, no more stuck keys, and an Android app that finds your PC by itself.

// FEATURES

Everything at your fingertips

A HUD for your tablet

Landing, power management, quantum, operator modes, shields, targeting, salvage, mining, doors, comms and emotes, on a HUD that fits your screen: one panel at a time on a phone, two halves on a tablet, and wide dashboards. Swipe with two fingers to flip panels. In English, Español, Français or Deutsch.

Cockpit controls

Slide a finger toward a face of the ship to shift shields, swipe across the targeting reticle, pull the ramp to open the doors, lift the cover off Panic Decoys. Pull the landing-gear lever, drag the power sliders and turn the operator-mode dial. Each step is one key press, at most five per drag.

Hold-to-talk voice

"Landing gear", "engage quantum", "shields up". Hold the mic button and speak. Windows' built-in speech recognition, offline.

12 manufacturer themes

RSI, Drake, Aegis, Anvil, Origin, MISC, Crusader, Banu, Aopoa, Argo, Consolidated Outland and Esperia, each its own design down to the levers, sliders and dials: Drake's riveted hazard plates, Origin's cream and black gloss, Argo's yellow work panels, Banu's lacquer on wood, and matching Stream Deck keys. With press animations, HUD sounds (on the tablet or in your headset), a night mode, large text and high contrast.

Layout editor

Drag buttons and clusters on a snapping grid that fits every screen, build your own dashboards, and change keys, labels, icons and animations. Share layouts as files, or pick a starter layout for Combat, Mining & Salvage, Hauling & Exploration, the Prospector or the Vulture.

Follows your keybinds

Reads your Star Citizen keybinds on any drive and in any channel, so rebinding in-game moves the button with it. An optional profile covers actions with no default key and keeps the binds you already have.

A proper Windows app

One installer. A window with the QR code, a tray icon, settings, start with Windows, and updates with one click.

Stream Deck

SpoolUp works with SpoolUp for Stream Deck: the plugin, icon pack and profiles for every Stream Deck from the Mini to the + XL, in all 12 manufacturer themes. Presses go through SpoolUp like a tablet's, and your tablet and Stream Deck can stay on the same scene. A separate paid product, available on the Elgato Marketplace.

// ANY TABLET, ANY PHONE

iPad, Android or iPhone

It runs in the browser, so there's nothing to install on the tablet. Scan the QR code, add it to your home screen, and it opens full screen like an app. The screen stays awake mid-flight.

// NEW IN 2.2

Battle alerts, more buttons, an Android app

SpoolUp reads the game's own log, and the deck shows when your ship is under fire and by whom, when it's disabled or destroyed, and your kills, in a quiet strip and a Last hits readout. 25 more buttons on Star Citizen 4.10's default keys: more and reset missiles, decoy bursts, target pins and forward cycles, radar ping, salvage heads, cargo jettison, light amplification and more. When the game loses focus, SpoolUp lets go of held keys. The keybind profile now keeps your own binds, SpoolUp finds the game on any drive and channel (or in the folder you pick in Settings), and the new Android app finds your PC on the network by itself, with an iPhone and iPad app on the way.

// FROM 2.1

Cockpit clusters, on any screen

Shields, targeting, lights and doors, turrets, countermeasures, the quantum spool and the mining and salvage heads are each one instrument you can place on any panel or dashboard, drawn by every maker its own way. Shields have no words: slide toward a face to shift strength, tap the ship to reset. Panels follow your screen, two-finger swipes flip them without ever pressing a button, and swiping up or down opens mobiGlas or Quick actions. Star the emotes you use to keep them a tap away.

// FROM 2.0

Levers, sliders and a dial

From the "Tablet Deck 2" design: hardware-style controls in every maker's style, from Drake's riveted hazard lever to Origin's cream knobs and Anvil's knurled dial. SpoolUp can't read the game, so the sliders show the steps it sent, and Center sliders resets them without pressing anything.

// LAYOUT EDITOR

Build your own deck

The editor shows the deck exactly as your tablet or phone does. Drag to reorder on a snapping grid, resize by whole cells, add clusters from a list or start a dashboard from a preset, pick keys from a list so they're always valid, and set colors, icons, press animations and voice phrases. Save, and your tablets switch to it by themselves.

// ON YOUR PC

One window, all the status

The app on your PC shows the QR code and pairing code to connect, and live telemetry: tablets connected, where keys go and the presses of the last minute. Voice status, settings and a log are a glance away, and it tucks into the tray while you fly.

// GET STARTED

Flying in three steps

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it on your gaming PC. It adds SpoolUp to the Start menu and, if you like, the Star Citizen keybind profile.

  2. Pair

    Open SpoolUp and scan its QR code with your tablet's camera, or type the address and pairing code.

  3. Fly

    Click into Star Citizen and tap away. Keys only go through while the game is the window in front.

// FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely, and the source is on GitHub under the MIT license. It's a fan project, and there's no paid version.

Is it a macro tool? Is it allowed?

It presses ordinary keyboard keys, one press per tap, like a second keyboard. A slider step, a lever pull or a dial turn is one press too. Keys go through while Star Citizen is the window in front.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

From 1.8.0 the installer is code-signed, so Windows shows its publisher. A brand-new release can still get this warning until enough people have downloaded it, and earlier releases aren't signed: click More info, then Run anyway. Every release lists the installer's checksum, and all the code is public on GitHub.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 on the gaming PC, and a tablet or phone with a browser on the same Wi-Fi or network. The voice commands need the English speech recognizer that comes with Windows.

Does it work on a Mac or Linux PC?

Not yet: the part that presses keys is Windows-only. The tablet can be anything with a browser.

What does it connect to?

The app checks GitHub for updates (you can turn that off), and the tablet loads the theme fonts from Google Fonts.
